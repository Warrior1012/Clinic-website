03 / The approach

Trust starts with transparency.

A clinic’s website should answer practical questions first: what is offered, who provides it, where to go and how to make contact.

Designed around people

Clarity is part of good care.

Northstar is a fictional clinic website concept created to demonstrate a clean, accessible patient-facing experience. It is not an operating healthcare provider.

  • No invented doctors, qualifications or testimonials
  • Service details must be verified by the real clinic
  • Appointment enquiries need a secure workflow

Before a real launch, add the legal business name, verified address, practitioner profiles and approved service descriptions.
